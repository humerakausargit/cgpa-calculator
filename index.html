Calculation Formulas & Standards

Semester GPA (SGPA):
SGPA = Σ (Course Credits × Grade Points) / Σ (Semester Credits)
Cumulative GPA (CGPA):
CGPA = Σ (Total Quality Points Earned) / Σ (Total Credits Attempted)
Percentage Equivalency:
10.0 Scale: Percentage (%) = CGPA × 9.5 (or [CGPA - 0.75] × 10)
